How to play

  1. Get from the start word to the goal word.
  2. Each step, change exactly one letter. Tap the letter, then tap its replacement. Every step must be a real word.
  3. Par is the fewest steps possible. Hit par with no hints for three stars.
  4. Green tiles already match the goal. Hard mode hides them.
Daily Climb. One four-letter ladder a day, the same for everyone.
Levels. Hand-picked ladders for 4, 5 and 6 letters. Clear one to unlock the next.
Endless. Random ladders, one after another. Giving up ends your run.
Sprint. Three minutes. Climb as many short ladders as you can. Hints cost 10 seconds, skips cost 15.
Par or Bust. Three lives. Each ladder finished over par, or skipped, costs a life. No hints.
Marathon. Long ladders of 7 steps or more.

On a keyboard, type to replace the selected letter, use the arrow keys to move, and press Backspace to undo.

Daily

One word a day, the same for everyone, in six guesses. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak.

Classic

  • Play word after word in one run. A miss ends the run.
  • Each solve scores 100 points plus 50 for every guess you have left. Every word in a row adds 10% more, up to double.
  • Easy: the 1,000 most common words, scored at ¾. Normal: any everyday word. Expert: hard mode rules and only five guesses, scored double.
  • You start each run with one hint and earn another every 3 words (hold up to 3). A hint shows one letter in place and costs 40 points.

Levels and badges

Every point you score is also XP, which raises your level. Daily solves earn a 100 XP bonus.

There are 24 badges, each with Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum tiers to work through. See them from the menu.

Answers are everyday words. Almost any real five-letter word is accepted as a guess.

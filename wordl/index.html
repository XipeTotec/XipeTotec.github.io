How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. Each guess must be a real word. After you guess, the tiles show how close you were.

Modes

  • Daily: one word a day, the same for everyone. Counts toward your streak.
  • Practice: as many words as you like.

Words come from the five-letter list in Rungs.
